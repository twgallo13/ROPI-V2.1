AOSS Product Editor Routes
========================

/products/:id - Product Editor Page
  - Tab: ?tab=core - Core Information
  - Tab: ?tab=attributes - Product Attributes
  - Tab: ?tab=descriptions - Descriptions & SEO
  - Tab: ?tab=launch - Launch & Media
  - Tab: ?tab=ai - AI Actions

Deep-linkable URLs:
- /products/123?tab=core
- /products/123?tab=attributes
- /products/123?tab=descriptions
- /products/123?tab=launch
- /products/123?tab=ai

Sidebar Panels (always visible):
- Observations Panel
- Smart Suggestions Panel
- Export Readiness Panel
